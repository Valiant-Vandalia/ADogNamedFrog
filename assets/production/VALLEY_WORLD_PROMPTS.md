## Shared production direction

- Fixed elevated southeast view looking northwest, consistent with the game camera.
- Flat hand-painted children’s picture-book treatment with broad soft brush texture, simplified shapes, light irregular outlines, and limited gentle shading.
- Book One is the visual authority. Avoid glossy 3D mobile-game rendering, photorealism, hyper-detail, blocked routes, extra entrances, characters, text, UI, and watermarks.
- Entire zone isolated on flat `#ff00ff`, with generous padding and no cast shadow outside the scene.
