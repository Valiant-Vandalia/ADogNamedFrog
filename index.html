The true story behind Frog

Before Frog, there was Sirius.

Sirius inspired the book’s joyful, big-hearted main character and the personality that bounds across every page.

What began with one beloved dog became a story about trying something new, finding your people, and discovering what you are capable of.

“The farm remembers every brave heart.”
