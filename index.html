The story

Sometimes courage starts with a little encouragement.

Follow a baby dog who discovers he can do something he never thought imaginable—with help from a friendly group of frogs.

Written for young readers and read-aloud moments, A Dog Named Frog celebrates self-belief, unexpected friendship, diversity, and the importance of the company we keep.

Meet the inspiration

Before Frog,
there was Sirius.

Josh’s dog Sirius inspired the book’s big-hearted main character. His joyful spirit and unmistakable personality helped bring Frog bounding onto the page.

The result is a warm adventure about trying something new, finding your people, and discovering what you’re capable of.

About the author

Joshua Allen Lanham

Joshua is a West Virginia native, West Virginia University alum, practicing attorney, and children’s book author.

He has shared A Dog Named Frog with classrooms throughout southern West Virginia and donated more than 100 copies to local elementary schools. The book was also featured on WSAZ’s Bookmark Monday.
